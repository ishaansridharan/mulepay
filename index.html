BouncePay

Payments that bounce until they connect.

Your phone doesn't need internet — your payment just needs a path to it. BouncePay bounces it from one nearby device to the next over Bluetooth, until it reaches one that is online.

How it works

The payment finds its own way out.

An encrypted packet is created offline, carried hop by hop over Bluetooth, and handed to the first device that can reach the internet.

1

Create payment

The buyer signs ₹250 offline. An encrypted packet is produced on the device.

2

Find nearby relay

BouncePay discovers phones in Bluetooth range — no internet required.

3

Store & forward

A relay holds the sealed packet and carries it until a better path appears.

4

Reach internet bridge

The first device with connectivity becomes the bridge to the network.

5

Settle payment

The gateway verifies the signature and settles. The receipt returns the same way.

For whom

Where a missing bar of signal costs a sale.

01

Crowded venues

Concerts, stadiums, festivals and expos, where thousands of phones congest the cell network at once.

02

Remote commerce

Remote shops, trekking routes, rural markets and camps where connectivity comes and goes.

03

Connectivity dead zones

Basements, back rooms and blind spots where neither side has signal — but someone a few metres away does.

BouncePay does not create connectivity out of nothing. It uses connectivity that already exists somewhere nearby, and carries the payment toward it — so a sale never depends on the one device holding the money.
